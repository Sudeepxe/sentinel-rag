# ADR-NNN: Title

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, business, constraints. State the problem being solved, not
the solution.

## Decision

What we decided, stated as a clear, active sentence.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or is a deliberate tradeoff as a result of this decision. Include
negative consequences honestly, not just benefits.
